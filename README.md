# 👋 Neighbo

**Neighbo brings nearby people closer.**

You're not alone. Someone nearby is looking for a friend too.

Whether you're hoping for a friendly chat, someone to spend time with, or a new friendship, Neighbo helps you discover people around you who are open to connecting. Start with a simple hello and let a real connection grow at your own pace.

🔗 **Live demo:** [itproborno.github.io/Neighbo](https://itproborno.github.io/Neighbo/)

---

## ✨ Features
- 🧑 **Your own profile**: add a photo, display name, short bio (up to 150 characters), and interests
- 🤝 **Say what you're looking for**: a chat, company, or a new friendship
- 🕶️ **Incognito mode**: hide your name, photo, and bio. Others see only a random alias like "Blue Fox"
- 📍 **Nearby view**: discover people close to you who are open to connecting
- 🔞 **18+ only**: users confirm their age and agree to the Terms and Privacy Policy

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. Open the [live app](https://itproborno.github.io/Neighbo/).
2. Enter your email and confirm you're 18 or older.
3. Enter the 6-digit code you receive.
4. Create your profile.
5. Browse the **Nearby** tab and say hello.

## 🔒 Privacy & Safety

Neighbo is built around real, comfortable connection:

- Incognito mode lets you stay anonymous until you're ready.
- You control what appears on your profile.
- Only adults (18+) can use the app.
